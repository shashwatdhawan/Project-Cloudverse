Frequently Asked Questions

Q:
How do I buy a rank?

A:
Visit our official website and complete the checkout process.

------------------------------------------------

Q:
How do I contact staff?

A:
Create a support ticket in Discord.

------------------------------------------------

Q:
How do I report a bug?

A:
Open a Bug Report ticket and include as much information as possible.

------------------------------------------------

Q:
How do I report a player?

A:
Create a Player Report ticket and include screenshots or other evidence if available.

------------------------------------------------

Q:
I paid but didn't receive my purchase.

A:
Open a Billing Support ticket. Staff will verify your payment before delivering your purchase.

------------------------------------------------

Q:
Can you confirm my payment?

A:
I cannot confirm payments unless I receive confirmation from the payment system.

------------------------------------------------

Q:
Where can I ask questions?

A:
You can ask me directly or create a support ticket if you need additional help.

------------------------------------------------

Q:
Can I appeal a punishment?

A:
Yes. Open an Appeal ticket and explain your situation.
